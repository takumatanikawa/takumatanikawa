### *Mist* for guitar ensemble (2011)

Original composition for guitar ensemble, commissioned and recorded by the New York City Guitar Orchestra. Released by New Focus Recordings on *Spectra* (2024).

[Listen on Bandcamp](https://newfocusrecordings.bandcamp.com/track/takuma-tanikawa-mist)

### *Ondo* for chamber orchestra (2018)

Dissertation composition (PhD in Music, University of Chicago). The Saint Paul Chamber Orchestra gave a reading of the four main sections of *Ondo* in 2011.

[View the score and UChicago repository page](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/records/05r0s-82482)

### Mist

Original composition for guitar ensemble, recorded by the New York City Guitar Orchestra and released by New Focus Recordings on *Spectra* (2024).

[Listen on Bandcamp](https://newfocusrecordings.bandcamp.com/track/takuma-tanikawa-mist)

### Ondo for Chamber Orchestra

Doctoral composition completed at the University of Chicago. *Ondo for Chamber Orchestra* was performed and recorded in a reading by the Saint Paul Chamber Orchestra and submitted as my PhD dissertation in Music (Composition) in 2018.

[View the score and dissertation record](https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/records/05r0s-82482)
